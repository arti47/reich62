## 1. HOW TO USE THIS BESTIARY

Each entry gives: **Type** (Minion/Rival/Nemesis/Animal/Encounter Block), a **Hook** (where/how it appears, tied to setting and Heat), **Characteristics**, **Derived stats**, **Skills**, **Talents/Abilities**, and **Equipment**. Minions have no strain track and share one Wound Threshold across the group (Manual §12C). Rivals and Nemeses follow standard rules. Reference Manual §12D for the special-ability definitions used below (Fear X, Hardened, etc.).

## 2. MINION GROUPS

### Checkpoint Guards
**Hook:** The default obstacle at any road, rail, or district boundary crossing. Usually 2–4 strong, bored, and looking for an excuse to escalate or extort a bribe.
- **Characteristics:** Br2 Ag2 In2 Cu1 Wi2 Pr1
- **Skills (group):** Perception, Coercion, Ranged
- **Wound Threshold:** 4 per member
- **Equipment:** P38 pistol or Kar98k rifle, whistle, papers-check stamp kit
- **Ability:** Papers-Check Reflex — automatically triggers a Personal Heat check (Manual §17.1) against any PC who fails a Deception/Cool check against them.

### Street Patrol (Ordnungspolizei)
**Hook:** Uniformed police walking a beat; the everyday face of surveillance in residential districts.
- **Characteristics:** Br2 Ag2 In2 Cu2 Wi2 Pr1
- **Skills (group):** Vigilance, Coercion, Streetwise
- **Wound Threshold:** 4 per member
- **Equipment:** Truncheon, P38 pistol, whistle
- **Ability:** Beat Familiarity — removes 🎲Setback from checks to notice unfamiliar faces/behavior in their own patrol district.

### SS Security Detail
**Hook:** Elite guards assigned to sensitive sites — Party buildings, rail depots carrying valuable cargo, detention facilities.
- **Characteristics:** Br3 Ag2 In2 Cu2 Wi3 Pr2
- **Skills (group):** Ranged, Vigilance, Discipline
- **Wound Threshold:** 5 per member
- **Equipment:** MP40 submachine gun, flak vest (Soak +2)
- **Ability:** Disciplined — immune to Disorient effects (Manual §9); won't break formation under pressure.

### Rail Yard Guards
**Hook:** Watch freight and passenger platforms; check manifests and travel permits.
- **Characteristics:** Br2 Ag2 In2 Cu2 Wi2 Pr1
- **Skills (group):** Perception, Knowledge, Ranged
- **Wound Threshold:** 4 per member
- **Equipment:** Kar98k rifle, manifest ledgers
- **Ability:** Manifest Cross-Check — 🎲Boost on Perception checks made specifically against forged travel papers (opposing Manual §15's forged-papers quality tiers).

### Factory Overseers
**Hook:** Enforce labor quotas and watch for sabotage or slowdown among conscripted/forced workers.
- **Characteristics:** Br2 Ag1 In2 Cu2 Wi2 Pr2
- **Skills (group):** Coercion, Perception, Mechanics
- **Wound Threshold:** 4 per member
- **Equipment:** Truncheon, clipboard/quota sheets
- **Ability:** Quota Pressure — a failed Coercion check against a worker under their watch adds 🎲Setback to that worker's next check this scene (fear of punishment).

### Border Patrol
**Hook:** Watches frontier crossings, forest tracks, and river approaches near contested/neutral borders.
- **Characteristics:** Br3 Ag2 In2 Cu2 Wi2 Pr1
- **Skills (group):** Survival, Perception, Ranged
- **Wound Threshold:** 5 per member
- **Equipment:** Kar98k rifle, flare pistol, tracking dog (see §5)
- **Ability:** Terrain-Wise — removes 🎲Setback from Survival/Perception checks made in their own patrol zone.

### Hitler Youth Watchers
**Hook:** Enthusiastic teenage informants and block wardens; low combat threat but high social/Heat danger due to sheer numbers and misplaced zeal.
- **Characteristics:** Br1 Ag2 In1 Cu1 Wi2 Pr1
- **Skills (group):** Vigilance, Streetwise
- **Wound Threshold:** 3 per member
- **Equipment:** None (unarmed)
- **Ability:** Everywhere — cannot be meaningfully avoided via Stealth in residential areas; instead, avoiding their notice requires a Deception/Charm check.

### Camp/Prison Guards
**Hook:** Staff detention facilities and labor camps; among the most dangerous minions due to willingness to use lethal force without hesitation.
- **Characteristics:** Br3 Ag2 In2 Cu2 Wi3 Pr1
- **Skills (group):** Ranged, Coercion, Vigilance
- **Wound Threshold:** 5 per member
- **Equipment:** Kar98k rifle, guard dog (see §5)

### Roadblock Soldiers (Wehrmacht)
**Hook:** Regular army units manning temporary roadblocks during troop movements or security sweeps.
- **Characteristics:** Br2 Ag2 In2 Cu1 Wi2 Pr1
- **Skills (group):** Ranged, Athletics, Vigilance
- **Wound Threshold:** 4 per member
- **Equipment:** Kar98k rifle, StG assault rifle (1 in 4)

### Informant Network (abstract minion)
**Hook:** Not a physical group but a standing web of low-level watchers — shopkeepers, neighbors, coworkers — represented mechanically rather than placed on a map.
- **Characteristics:** N/A (no combat stats — social/information hazard only)
- **Ability:** Passive Watch — at the start of any scene in a populated area, the GM may secretly roll to see if the Informant Network notices something (treat as an Oracle roll, Manual §18, "Unlikely" by default, more likely if the PCs have raised Heat recently).

## 6. ABSTRACT ENCOUNTER BLOCKS

These are not individual NPCs but ready-to-use obstacle templates — a full "encounter" reduced to a check or short sequence, for fast GM deployment.

### Checkpoint (as encounter)
**Hook:** The most common Reich '62 obstacle; can be resolved in seconds or become a full scene depending on complications.
- **Resolution:** Opposed Deception/Cool vs. Perception (guard's rating: 2🎲, per Checkpoint Guards minion profile). Failure → Personal Heat check per Manual §17.1; failure with 🔻🔻🔻/⚡ → immediate escalation to a Papers-Checked or Tailed threshold effect regardless of current Heat level.

### Search Detail / House Search
**Hook:** A team arrives to search a residence, vehicle, or person on suspicion.
- **Resolution:** Opposed Skulduggery (concealment prepared beforehand) vs. Perception (searchers' rating: 2–3🎲 depending on thoroughness). A Safehouse Kit (Manual §15) or Hidden Document Pouch (Manual §15) grants 🎲Boost. Failure reveals whatever was hidden; treat consequences per GM/Heat rules.

### Manhunt / Dragnet
**Hook:** A coordinated, area-wide search following a serious incident (a killing, a successful sabotage, a high-profile escape).
- **Resolution:** A multi-round extended opposed check — Stealth/Streetwise vs. a scaling Perception pool (starts at 2🎲, +1🎲 per hour the dragnet continues, capped at 4🎲). Each failed round advances Personal/Cell Heat by 1. Escaping the search zone entirely ends the dragnet.

### Interrogation (as encounter)
**Hook:** A captured PC or NPC being questioned; uses the Social Encounter subsystem (Manual §11) directly.
- **Resolution:** Opposed Discipline vs. the interrogator's Coercion (use Gestapo Interrogator or "Herr Wolf" ratings above for severity). Apply Manual §11's Advantage/Threat spend tables in full; a Despair result may reveal a name, location, or plan the GM can use as a complication in a future session.
